Privacy Policy

Ladder Metronome (Android) · Developer: LadderMetro · Effective date: October 20, 2026

In short: Ladder Metronome has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your settings stay on your phone. The only thing the app sends over the internet is a technical check for app updates, described below.

1. Who we are

This policy applies to the Ladder Metronome app for Android (package name com.ladder.metronome), published on Google Play by the developer LadderMetro ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at laddermetroapp@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

The app saves your preferences on your phone, such as tempo, theme, sound pack, timer and Ramp settings, and audio latency calibration. This data never leaves your device through the app. We cannot see it.

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include these preferences in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

3. Data sent over the internet: app updates

To deliver bug fixes and small improvements quickly, the app checks for updates when it starts. This uses the update service of Expo (650 Industries, Inc.), which acts as our service provider. Each check sends:

  • Your device's operating system and app version information
  • A random installation identifier created by the app (it is not your name, phone number, email, or advertising ID)
  • The app's project identifier

As with any internet connection, the update server also sees your device's IP address. We do not use this information to identify you, to determine your location, for advertising, or to build a profile. It is used only to deliver the correct update. The connection is encrypted (HTTPS). Expo processes this data under its own privacy policy: expo.dev/privacy.

4. What we do not do

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising and no advertising ID
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No access to your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, files, or location
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties

6. Children

Ladder Metronome is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Retention and deletion

Data stored on your device remains until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Uninstalling also discards the random installation identifier. Data received by the update service is kept according to Expo's privacy policy. For any request about your data, email us at the address in section 1.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your data, for example a future paid version or new features, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
